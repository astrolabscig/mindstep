Worries love to loop... especially at night. Here's the move. Write the worry down in one sentence. Naming it shrinks it. Ask: can I do anything about this today? If yes... do that one thing. If no... give it an appointment. Ten minutes tomorrow afternoon. When it comes back tonight, tell it: noted... two pm. Then put your attention back on what you're doing. Out loud if you have to. You're not ignoring it. You're giving it a time and place.
