After a frightening or violent event... the body can stay on guard. Flashbacks. Nightmares. Numbness. Avoiding reminders. Feeling jumpy or detached. These are common trauma responses. Not weakness. Not madness. Trauma can heal with safety and support. Focus on present safety. Keep one grounding routine. Tell a trusted person what helps and what does not. Ask for trauma-informed care. You can say no to details you do not want to share. And if symptoms persist, or you feel unsafe, contact a professional or the helpline. You do not have to retell everything to deserve help.
