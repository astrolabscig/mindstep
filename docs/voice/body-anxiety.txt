Sometimes your heart races... your chest tightens... and your mind jumps to the worst. Often that's your body bracing for a threat... it's uncomfortable, but it passes. Help it along. Sit down. Feet flat. One hand on your chest. Breathe out slowly... longer than you breathe in. Say kindly: this is my body bracing... it will pass. And if it keeps happening, or you're worried it's something physical, see a nurse or doctor. Getting checked is the smart move.
