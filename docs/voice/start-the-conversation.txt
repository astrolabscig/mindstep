You don't need perfect words to check on someone. You need a calm moment... one honest observation... and good ears. Try a walk — side by side beats face to face. Start with what you've seen: you've seemed quiet lately... how are you, really? Then listen more than you fix. Nods help. "That sounds hard" helps. Advice can wait. End with one question: what would help, even a little? That's it. That's the whole script.
