Eating disorders involve serious changes in eating... body image... and control. They are not vanity. They can affect anyone... not just young women. And they are treatable. Early help reduces harm. Tell one trusted person and a health professional. Ask for a medical check, because restriction and purging can affect the heart and electrolytes. Avoid diet talk... scales... and body comments around the person. And if there is fainting... chest pain... or severe restriction, seek urgent care. Food should not be a daily source of fear or shame.
