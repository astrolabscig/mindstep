Substance use becomes a health issue when drinking or drug use is hard to control... causes harm... or is needed to get through the day. It is not a moral failure. It is a health issue. And treatment works. Notice the pattern. What do you use? How often? What does it replace? Do not stop alcohol or sedatives suddenly without medical advice. Some withdrawals can be dangerous. Call the helpline or a health centre for options. Tell one person who can check in without shame. Getting help is safer than stopping alone.
