Anxiety becomes a health issue when the alarm will not switch off. When worry... fear... or avoidance is intense... lasts... and gets in the way of daily life. Panic can feel physical. Racing heart... tight chest... dizziness. It is frightening. It is not usually dangerous. Still, if physical symptoms are new or worrying, get checked. And anxiety is treatable. Write down what you avoid because of fear. Practise one small exposure with a safe person. Use slow exhales. Reduce caffeine and sleep debt. And if panic keeps returning, or you stop normal activities, seek professional help. Small steps and support help.
