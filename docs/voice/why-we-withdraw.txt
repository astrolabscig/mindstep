Low mood does not always look like crying. Sometimes it looks like cancelling plans... sleeping more... or snapping at the people closest to you. That can seem like rudeness. Often it is exhaustion and shame. If this is you, check the basics. Sleep. Food. Water. Daylight. Send one low-pressure message: I am not okay today. No need to fix it. If it is someone you care about, offer specific company. Walk with me at five? Do not ask them to organise it. And if the low mood or withdrawal lasts two weeks or more, encourage a health centre visit.
