The myths are loud. Mental illness is weakness... it's a curse... it's contagious. None of that is true. It's a health condition... and people recover and contribute, like anyone else. You don't need to win debates. Answer fear with a person: Ama has depression... and she's still the best student in our class. Say a person living with... never a mad person. Share what you know in small doses. One honest conversation at a time. That's how stigma loses.
