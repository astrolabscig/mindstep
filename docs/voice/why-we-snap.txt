When your brain senses a threat... your body picks a survival move. Fight... flight... freeze... or fawn. That can look like snapping... running... going blank... or agreeing to everything. It is not a character flaw. It is an alarm. So notice the signal. Hot face... tight chest... raised voice... or going numb. Name it silently: my alarm is on. Then buy time. Slow your exhale. Put both feet on the floor. Delay the next sentence. And afterwards, repair. I reacted strongly. I am sorry. Can we try that again? That is how the alarm loses its grip.
