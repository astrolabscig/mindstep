Can't sleep? Stop fighting the bed. Sleep comes to a quiet mind in a dark, cool room. Protect it like this. Keep one wake-up time... even weekends. It anchors your body clock. Before bed, park tomorrow — write your list, then leave it. Screens away thirty to sixty minutes early... that light tells your brain it's daytime. And if you're still awake after twenty minutes, get up. Do something boring in dim light. Come back when you're sleepy.
