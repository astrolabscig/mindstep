Some days feel flat... like everything is behind glass. If that's today, start small. Step outside for five minutes. Sunlight, air, a short walk. That's the whole task. Then do one small thing you used to enjoy — music... a call... a meal you like. And tell one trusted person how today really was. Not "I'm fine"... the truth. If this flatness has stayed for two weeks or more, visiting a health centre is a wise move. Not dramatic. Wise.
