When the work piles up, your mind keeps repeating everything at once... and that's exhausting. So trick it. Grab paper and dump every task onto one list. All of them. Two minutes. Now circle the one thing due first... and give it just ten minutes. You can stop after ten. You probably won't want to. And here's the part people forget: tell someone. A friend, a lecturer. Deadlines bend more often than you think.
