Helping a friend matters... and so do you. You're a friend, not a therapist. Even therapists have supervisors. So decide what you can honestly offer. Listening... company... walking with them to a clinic. Help them reach real support. Offer to sit beside them while they call. And keep your own rest, meals, and study time. Cancelling all of it helps no one. If you're ever worried about their safety, tell a trusted adult or a professional. That's not betrayal. That's loyalty.
