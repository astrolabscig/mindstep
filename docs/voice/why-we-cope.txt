Alcohol... overwork... scrolling... gambling... isolation. They can all be ways to escape pain. And they work for an hour. Then they add a new problem. That does not make you weak. It makes you human. So get curious, not ashamed. Name what the behaviour helps you avoid. Loneliness? Failure? Memories? Boredom? Then choose one lower-cost swap for tonight. A walk. A call. A shower. A meal. Tell one person the honest version. And if you need more to feel okay, or stopping feels dangerous, get professional help. Coping can change.
