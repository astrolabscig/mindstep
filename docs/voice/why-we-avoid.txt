Avoiding something scary works... for a moment. The relief is real. But your brain learns: that worked. So the fear grows. Maybe you postpone the call... the assignment... the conversation. Each time, the dread gets heavier. The way out is not waiting to feel ready. It is a small step toward the fear. Write down what you are avoiding. Make the first step almost silly. Open the email. Walk past the room. Then stay with the discomfort for sixty seconds. Repeat. The fear learns from experience, not from a pep talk.
