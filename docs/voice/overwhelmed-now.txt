If everything feels like too much right now... that's okay. Let's slow it down. Breathe in for four... and out for six. Again. Five times if you can. Now look around you. Name five things you can see... four sounds you can hear... three things you can touch. Let your shoulders drop. Let your feet press the floor. You don't have to solve anything yet. If someone safe is nearby, tell them you're feeling overwhelmed. Saying it out loud... already helps.
