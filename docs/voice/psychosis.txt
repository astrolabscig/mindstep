Psychosis can involve hearing or seeing things others do not... unusual beliefs... confusion... or withdrawal. It can be frightening for the person and for those around them. A person is not mad. Not dangerous because of psychosis. And it is treatable. Stay calm. Reduce noise and crowds. Do not argue about the experience. Ask what would help them feel safe. Contact a health professional or the helpline. If there is danger, call one-one-two. And do not leave someone alone if they are confused or at risk. Early help matters.
