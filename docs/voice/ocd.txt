OCD is not just liking things tidy. It involves unwanted intrusive thoughts... and rituals or checking that try to reduce the fear. Maybe a thought keeps returning. Maybe you wash... count... check... or ask for reassurance. The ritual gives brief relief. Then the cycle starts again. That is exhausting. And it is treatable. Name the loop: thought... fear... ritual... relief. Delay one ritual by two minutes. Notice the fear peak... and the fall. Ask for CBT with exposure and response prevention. Do not force someone to stop all rituals at once. Get support.
