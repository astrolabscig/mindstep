Bipolar disorder is not ordinary mood swings. It involves episodes of low mood... and periods of unusually high energy... irritability... or a much smaller need for sleep. The shifts can last days or longer. They can affect judgement... money... relationships... safety. Professional assessment matters, because treatment is different from ordinary low mood. Track mood... sleep... and energy for a few weeks. Do not stop or start medicines without a clinician. Plan early warning signs with someone you trust. And if someone is unsafe, or not sleeping for days, seek urgent help.
