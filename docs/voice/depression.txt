Depression is more than sadness. It can bring weeks of low mood... loss of interest... changes in sleep... appetite... energy... focus... or movement. It is a health condition. Not laziness. Not a character flaw. And it is treatable. So note how long it has lasted. Notice how it affects work... study... relationships. Tell one trusted person. Book a health centre or counselling visit. Keep one small routine: food... water... sleep... daylight. And if you have thoughts of harming yourself, call the Mental Health Authority on oh-eight-hundred, six seven eight, six seven eight. Or one-one-two. Today counts.
